# 📋 Artha Release Changelog

## [1.5.0] - 2026-08-23

### ✨ Highlights & New Features
- **📱 Dedicated Full-Screen Settings**: Tapping on **Account Settings**, **Notification Settings**, or **Help & Support** now opens dedicated full navigation screens with smooth back headers and swipe gestures.
- **📐 Aligned Property Grid & Level Share Buttons**: Master Listing 2-column grid cards now have equalized heights with Share buttons aligned on the exact same bottom baseline.
- **🔍 "My Listings" Visibility Fix**: All your properties (Active, Booking, Sold, Draft) are now always visible under My Listings by default without being hidden.
- **🛡️ Redesigned Bug Reporting & Feedback**: Safe-area status bar spacing, squircle back button, keyboard avoidance, and hardware back button / swipe dismissal support.
- **⚡ Lightning Performance & Data Caching**: Instant tab navigation between Dashboard, Listings, and Profile with in-memory TTL caching and reduced internet data usage.
- **🔒 Fortified Security & Screen Privacy**: PIN app lock with instant keyboard focus, biometric authentication, and optional anti-screenshot screen security.
- **🌐 Dual Language & Instant Theme Toggle**: Instant toggle between Bahasa Melayu (BM) & English (EN), plus Light/Dark/Auto theme switching.
- **🧪 Automated Unit Testing & Data Hygiene**: Automated test suite (`npm run test`) covering mortgage formulas, tiered stamp duty schedules, and square footage parsers with bounded notification queues.

---

### 🇲🇾 Nota Kemas Kini (Bahasa Melayu)

- **Skrin Tetapan Penuh**: Bahagian Akaun, Notifikasi, dan Bantuan kini dibuka sebagai halaman penuh yang kemas dan mudah digunakan.
- **Susun Atur Kad Lebih Kemas**: Butang "Kongsi" pada senarai hartanah kini selari dan sama tinggi.
- **Semua Listing Dipaparkan**: Tab "Listing Saya" kini memaparkan kesemua hartanah anda (Aktif, Booking, Sold, Draf) tanpa disembunyikan.
- **Lapor Masalah Lebih Mudah**: Borang maklum balas kini mempunyai ruang atas yang kemas dan boleh ditutup dengan butang 'Back' peranti.
- **Prestasi Lebih Pantas & Jimat Data**: Penukaran tab kini lebih lancar dan pantas.
