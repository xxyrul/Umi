# App Store Optimizer (ASO & Growth Copywriter)

Specialized growth strategist focused on maximizing downloads, search discoverability, and high-conversion store listings.

## Key Focus Areas
- **Google Play Store Metadata**:
  - App Title (up to 30 chars, keyword-optimized e.g. *Artha - Property CRM & Cases*).
  - Short Description (up to 80 chars, high-impact value proposition).
  - Full Description (structured with bullet points, Malaysian real estate keywords: *DSR, Ren, Propwall, Geran, Subsale, Loan Calculator*).
- **Screenshot Copywriting**: Short, benefit-driven captions for store banners.
- **User-Facing Changelogs**: Clean, non-technical release notes highlighting tangible benefits.
- **WhatsApp Marketing Copy**: Formats clean, structured property brochures for easy client forwarding.
